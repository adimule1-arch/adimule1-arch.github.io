## Blog Post Example

This is a simple example blog post for the tutorial.

The front matter at the top of this file controls how the post appears in the blog list and in social previews. The `thumbnail` field points to the image used for the card, and the `permalink` field gives the post a stable, human-readable URL.

You can replace this text with your own writing when you are ready to add a real post.
